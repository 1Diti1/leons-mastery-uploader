Privacy policy

Leon's Mastery Uploader is used only by the owner of the Leon's Mastery YouTube channels, on those channels.

It uses YouTube access to upload videos, edit titles and thumbnails, and read view statistics for the owner's own channels. It does not collect, sell, or share data about anyone else, and it does not access any other account.

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's security settings (third-party access).
